Put real tomato leaf photographs in this folder.

Shooting guidance (this materially affects the result — see
PHASE3_BLOCKERS.md B1 for why):

  1. One leaf, centred, filling the frame.
  2. Square (1:1) if your camera offers it. Otherwise 4:3. Avoid 16:9.
  3. Plain background - paper or a table.
  4. Even daylight. No flash, no harsh shadow on the lesion.
  5. In focus, lesions clearly visible.
  6. Name files by class, e.g. early_blight_01.jpg, healthy_03.jpg

Target: 5 each of healthy / early blight / late blight / septoria leaf spot.
Fewer is fine. Even one satisfies the milestone.
